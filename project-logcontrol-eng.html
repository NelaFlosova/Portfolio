LogControl

Animation video for a security tool

Role
Motion Designer
Company
ORBIT s.r.o.
Product
LogControl (Security Product)
Focus
Motion Design, Animation, Promo
Mission

Motion design for complex data

LogControl is a powerful tool for centralized log management, but explaining it with static screenshots or text is difficult. ORBIT needed a promo video that would do the heavy lifting—explaining the core features and value proposition quickly while keeping the viewer engaged.

The Concept

Instead of abstract shapes, I animated the actual product interface. The video serves as a dynamic walkthrough, simulating real interactions—like navigating through logs and filtering data. This gives potential clients a genuine feel for the tool's workflow and speed before they even book a demo.

Result

A clear visual pitch

The video translates complex features into an accessible visual story. It allows the sales team to explain the product's value in under a minute, delivering key information in a digestible way without overwhelming the viewer.
