Anni.Care • Website | Motion Design

Designing a Smarter Web Experience for Healthcare Providers

Role
UX/UI Designer, Front-end Developer
Length
6 months, 2023
Tools
Figma, React, GSAP
Mission

Creating clarity in healthcare technology

Joining anni.care meant stepping into a space where healthcare meets technology. Working closely with product managers, I aligned with the team's vision of simplifying medical equipment services for both B2C and B2B users.

DISCOVERY

Understanding the complexity

In my first weeks, I immersed myself in understanding the subscription-based calculation system at the heart of the customer experience. The platform's subscription logic had multiple touchpoints with user-facing flows, requiring a clear translation into streamlined and intuitive experience.

PLANNING

Planning the Interface

My first task was to define how this complexity could be translated into a streamlined experience. I mapped out user flows in Figma, focusing on clarity, transparency, and guidance — ensuring users could easily understand their subscription tiers, pricing logic, and next steps.

UI DESIGN

Responsive high-fidelity screens

With core flows defined, I designed responsive high-fidelity screens in Figma. Drawing on anni.care's existing brand foundations, I extended the visual language with clean, functional layouts that put usability first. Accessibility and user trust were top of mind, especially in a product so closely tied to healthcare services.

RESULTS

Impact

A foundation for growth

This project brought together UX thinking and web design into a single, coherent system. It gave anni.care a solid digital foundation to scale its services and communicate more effectively with clients. The result is a platform built with intention, and designed to last.
