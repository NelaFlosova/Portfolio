Turning security training into a clear product

Role

ORBIT s. r. o.
Product
PhishControl (Web Platform)
Focus
UX/UI Design, Visual Identity, User Journey
Overview

A cleaner take on phishing simulation

PhishControl is a platform that lets companies run simulated phishing campaigns to train their employees. I was responsible for the visual style and UX/UI design across the entire web experience. Instead of using heavy cybersecurity jargon or intimidating visuals, I kept things straightforward. I designed a cohesive user journey that starts with a clear, engaging landing page and transitions smoothly into the actual product dashboard, where admins can easily set up and manage their campaigns.
