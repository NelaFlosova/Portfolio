Role

Company
Practice project
Product
3D architecture render
Focus
3D Modeling, Architectural Design, Visualization
Mission

From concept to final render

This project was a comprehensive exercise in full-stack 3D production. I designed the architecture and modeled the structure entirely from scratch in Blender, ensuring the geometry was custom-built rather than assembled from assets. Once the form was established, I focused on achieving photorealism through material creation and atmospheric lighting. The scene combines my architectural design skills with technical rendering, using volumetric fog and detailed texturing to ground the original structure in a believable, cinematic environment.
